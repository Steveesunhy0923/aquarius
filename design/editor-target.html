The Mean Value Theorem

If f is continuous on the closed interval [a, b] and differentiable on the open interval (a, b), then there exists at least one point c ∈ (a, b) such that the instantaneous rate of change equals the average rate of change:

(1)f′(c) = f(b) − f(a)b − a

Geometrically, there is a point where the tangent line is parallel to the secant through the endpoints. A direct consequence: if f′(x) = 0 for all x in an interval, then f is constant there.
